How it hides

            
PNG / BMP · colour values change by ±1 in busy areas only; matrix embedding alters at most 1 value in 7.

            
JPEG · quantization index modulation on the compressed DCT coefficients, with error correction.

            
Text · zero-width Unicode characters after the first word: 2 bits each, or 1 bit each in Arabic text.

            
Lock · AES-256-GCM; the key comes from your password through scrypt.
